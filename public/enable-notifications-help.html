1 Find the lock icon

  1. Look at the address bar (URL bar) at the top of your browser
  2. You'll see a 🔒 lock icon or ⓘ info icon on the left side, just before the URL
  3. Click on it

2 Open site settings

  1. In the popup menu, click "Site settings"
  2. A new tab will open showing permissions for this site

3 Enable notifications

  1. Scroll down and find "Notifications"
  2. Click on the dropdown that says "Block"
  3. Change it to "Allow"

4 Reload the page

  1. Close the site settings tab
  2. Go back to the admin dashboard
  3. Press F5 or Ctrl+R to reload
  4. You should see a success message!
